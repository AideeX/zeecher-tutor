## Content ownership and provenance policy

1. Official NERDC PDFs may be consulted to construct original lessons and paraphrased objectives with source links; do not paste large copyrighted passages or republish official PDFs in app content.
2. Do not download/paywall-scrape commercial publishers' entrance papers. Questions must be original unless parent supplies lawfully usable material.
3. Maintain an auditable objective mapping: source URL, version, grade, section or page *only if verified*, verification date, and `provenance` status.
4. Distinguish **official syllabus content**, **legacy matched teaching content**, and **additional reasoning/entrance-enrichment**. Reasoning categories are original pedagogy taxonomies, not official stand-alone NERDC curricula.
5. A working 432-topic inventory exists locally. Its count describes the initial instructional map, *not confirmed exhaustive current-curriculum coverage*.
